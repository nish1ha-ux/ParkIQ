# ParkIQ Visitor Parking Regulations

1. Designated Visitor Slots:
   - Visitor slots are marked V-01 to V-10, located on Level 1 near the main lobby entrance.
2. Visitor Check-In Registration:
   - All visitors must scan their digital check-in QR code at the information desk or scan the visitor kiosk at check-in.
3. Free Validation Program:
   - Retail and shopping mall patrons can validate parking at participating merchants.
   - Validated tickets receive up to 2 hours of free parking. Subsequent hours are billed at the standard rate of $6.50/hour.
4. Maximum Stay Limit:
   - General visitor parking is subject to a maximum continuous stay limit of 4 hours. Vehicles parked longer without validation or permission are subject to warning notices and possible towing.
