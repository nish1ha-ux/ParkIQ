# ParkIQ Parking Facility Maps & Layouts

1. Level 1: Contains specialized slots, including:
   - Slots EV-01 to EV-10: Electric vehicle charging bays equipped with Level 2 chargers.
   - Slots H-01 to H-05: Designated handicap accessible slots located adjacent to the elevator lobby and main entrance.
   - Slots V-01 to V-10: Visitor parking area.
2. Level 2: Standard vehicle parking slots (S-01 to S-50). Intended for long-term and standard vehicle parking.
3. Level 3 (Rooftop): Open-air roof deck parking slots (R-01 to R-50). Recommended for oversized vehicles.
4. Access Points:
   - Entrance Gate A: Located on the North Boulevard. Includes automated check-in scanner.
   - Exit Gate B: Located on the South Avenue. Includes checkout terminal and payment terminal.
   - Ramps: One-way ramps link Level 1 to Level 2 and Level 2 to Level 3.
   - Elevators and Stairs: Located in the northeast corner of the building on all floors.
