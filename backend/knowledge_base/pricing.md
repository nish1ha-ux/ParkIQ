# ParkIQ Pricing & Payment Guidelines

1. Base Rates:
   - Standard hourly rate: $6.50 per hour or part thereof.
   - Daily maximum cap: $35.00 for any continuous 24-hour period.
2. Dynamic Surge Pricing:
   - When overall parking lot occupancy exceeds 80%, a dynamic multiplier of up to 1.5x may be applied to the base rate.
3. Special Charging Rates:
   - EV Charging bays: $0.25 per kWh consumed in addition to the hourly parking rate.
   - Idle fees: If a vehicle remains connected to an EV charger after charging is 100% complete, an idle fee of $0.10 per minute applies starting 15 minutes after full charge notification.
4. Overstay Penalties:
   - Overstaying past your reserved time or estimated departure by more than 15 minutes incurs an overstay fee of $10.00 per hour.
5. Lost Ticket or Session QR Issue:
   - If a session cannot be verified, a flat lost ticket fee of $45.00 will be assessed at Exit Gate B.
