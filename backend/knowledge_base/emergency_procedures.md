# ParkIQ Emergency & Assistance Procedures

1. Emergency Contacts:
   - For all security, breakdown, or medical emergencies, call the ParkIQ Helpline at 1-800-PARKIQ-HELP (1-800-727-5474) or dial 911.
2. Blue Assistance Buttons:
   - Emergency intercom assistance buttons are located at every elevator bank, stairwell entrance, and near payment kiosks. Press and hold for 3 seconds to be connected directly to dispatch.
3. Vehicle Breakdown:
   - If a vehicle breaks down inside the garage, switch on hazard lights. Do not block lanes. Contact assistance or notify parking staff immediately. Towing assistance is available on-site.
4. Evacuation Protocol:
   - In case of fire or structural emergency, strobe lights will flash and sirens will sound.
   - Proceed calmly to the nearest stairwell exit. Do not use elevators.
   - Assembly Point A: Open area outside Entrance Gate A.
   - Assembly Point B: Open plaza outside Exit Gate B.
