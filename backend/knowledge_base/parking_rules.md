# Official ParkIQ Parking Rules & Regulations

1. Speed Limit: Maximum vehicle speed limit inside all ParkIQ parking facilities is 10 km/h.
2. Slot Centering: All vehicles must be parked centered between marked white slot lines.
3. AES-256 QR Identity: Drivers must present their AES-256 encrypted digital QR code at entry and exit gates.
4. Overstay Penalties: Overstaying past your reserved or estimated parking duration incurs a penalty fee of $10.00 per hour.
5. No Unauthorized Parking: Parking in designated EV charging bays without active charging or in Handicap spots without a valid permit is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate towing.
