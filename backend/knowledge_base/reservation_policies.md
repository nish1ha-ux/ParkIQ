# ParkIQ Booking & Reservation Policies

1. Pre-Booking Window:
   - Reservations can be booked up to 7 days in advance.
   - The minimum reservation duration is 1 hour.
2. Cancellation and Refund Policy:
   - Cancellations requested 30 minutes or more before the reservation start time will receive a 100% refund.
   - Cancellations made less than 30 minutes before the start time will incur a 20% cancellation fee, with the remaining 80% refunded to the payment method.
3. Grace Period & Holds:
   - Reserved slots are held for up to 15 minutes past the scheduled arrival time. If the vehicle check-in is not recorded within this grace period, the booking is automatically cancelled as a no-show.
4. Duration Extensions:
   - Drivers can extend an active parking reservation via the mobile app in increments of 15, 30, or 60 minutes, subject to slot availability.
